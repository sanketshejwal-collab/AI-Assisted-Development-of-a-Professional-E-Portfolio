Task 1: Comprehensive Baseline Cartographic Layout

Importance of Baseline Cartographic Layout:

  • To standardize spatial data presentation so maps are instantly clear, accurate, and actionable for decision-makers.
  • To have a strong visual hierarchy—map canvas, clear title, scale bar, north arrow, legend, graticule/grid, and basic metadata.
  • Ensures technical accuracy, enables reproducibility across reports, and bridges the gap between complex GIS analysis and stakeholder understanding.

Key Learnings:

  • Visual Balance: Dominant thematic features (e.g., pressure points) stand out; background basemaps stay muted.
  • Intuitive Color Scheme: Hot colors for impact/risk, cool colors for natural features like water.
